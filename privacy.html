POPIA Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · South Africa · Testing Phase · Last updated 20 November 2026

1. Introduction

Kaiend Care is committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of all patients and healthcare professionals using the application.

This Privacy Policy explains how Kaiend Care collects, uses, stores and protects personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

2. Information We Collect

Kaiend Care may collect the following information:

Patient information

  • Full names, surname, age and contact details.
  • Medical conditions and health history.
  • Blood pressure and blood glucose readings.
  • Weight and BMI.
  • Current medication and adherence records.
  • Reported symptoms and relevant laboratory results.
  • Appointment and communication records.

Healthcare professional information

  • Full names and contact details.
  • Identification documents.
  • HPCSA or SANC registration numbers, where applicable.
  • Professional credentials and verification records.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected is used to:

  • Monitor chronic health conditions.
  • Support medication adherence.
  • Facilitate communication between patients and healthcare professionals.
  • Allow authorised clinicians to review patient health information.
  • Manage appointments and follow-ups.
  • Provide health education and dietary support.
  • Verify healthcare professional identities.
  • Improve application security and functionality.

4. Information Sharing

Patient information may be shared with the healthcare practice and authorised healthcare professionals responsible for managing the patient's chronic care.

Information will not be made available to unauthorised individuals or sold to third parties for commercial purposes.

Third-party service providers may process information where necessary to operate and maintain the application, subject to appropriate privacy and security safeguards.

5. Data Protection and Security

Kaiend Care is committed to implementing reasonable technical and organisational security measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

Access to patient information is restricted to authorised individuals with legitimate access.

Where a security compromise occurs, Kaiend Care will follow applicable POPIA requirements, including notifying the Information Regulator and affected individuals where required.

6. Your Rights Under POPIA

Users have the right to:

  • Request access to their personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion where legally permissible.
  • Withdraw consent where consent is the applicable lawful basis.
  • Object to certain forms of information processing.
  • Raise privacy concerns or lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.

Certain information may need to be retained to comply with applicable legal and healthcare record-keeping obligations.
