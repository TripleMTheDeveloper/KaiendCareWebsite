Terms of Use

Version 1.0 · South Africa · Testing Phase

1. Purpose of the Application

Kaiend Care is a digital chronic disease monitoring and healthcare support application designed to assist patients living with hypertension, diabetes, hyperlipidaemia and obesity.

The application allows users to record health measurements, monitor medication adherence, report symptoms, request appointments and share relevant health information with authorised healthcare professionals.

2. Medical Disclaimer

Kaiend Care is not a diagnostic application and does not independently provide medical diagnoses or medical advice.

The application does not replace consultations with registered healthcare professionals, prescribed treatment or emergency medical services.

Patients must not change or discontinue prescribed medication based on information displayed in the application.

3. Patient Responsibilities

Patients agree to:

  • Provide accurate personal and medical information.
  • Record blood pressure, glucose, weight and other health measurements honestly.
  • Keep medication information updated.
  • Report symptoms accurately.
  • Use the application responsibly.
  • Seek professional medical attention when necessary.

4. Healthcare Professional Verification

Healthcare professionals must undergo verification before accessing the professional dashboard.

Verification may require:

  • Valid identification.
  • HPCSA registration number, where applicable.
  • SANC registration number, where applicable.
  • Relevant professional credentials.

Kaiend Care's authorised administration will review submitted credentials to prevent impersonation and unauthorised professional access.

Healthcare professionals remain responsible for their clinical decisions, professional conduct and patient confidentiality.

5. Patient Information and Data Sharing

Kaiend Care collects personal and health information, including patient details, medical conditions, blood pressure, glucose readings, BMI, medication records and reported symptoms.

Information may be shared with the patient's authorised healthcare practice or professional for legitimate healthcare monitoring and management purposes, subject to applicable consent and other lawful processing requirements.

Kaiend Care handles personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

Users have rights regarding access to, correction of and protection of their personal information.

6. Messaging and Appointments

The messaging feature facilitates communication between patients and their authorised healthcare professionals.

Messages are not a substitute for formal medical consultations.

Appointment requests are subject to confirmation by the relevant healthcare practice.

Kaiend Care does not guarantee immediate responses from healthcare professionals.

7. Emergency Situations

Kaiend Care is not an emergency response service and does not guarantee real-time monitoring or immediate clinical intervention.

Patients experiencing severe or potentially life-threatening symptoms must seek emergency medical assistance immediately.

Do not wait for an application notification or healthcare professional response.

8. Application Limitations During Testing

This version of Kaiend Care is undergoing testing.

Users acknowledge that certain features may experience technical errors, incomplete functionality, delayed notifications, data synchronisation problems or temporary interruptions.

Users should not rely exclusively on the application for important medical records, medication reminders or emergency monitoring.

Any technical issues should be reported to the Kaiend Care development or administration team.

9. Responsible Use

Users must not provide false information, impersonate healthcare professionals, access unauthorised accounts or misuse another person's medical information.

Kaiend Care reserves the right to restrict access where fraudulent activity, security concerns or misuse is identified.

10. Acceptance

By selecting "I Agree", I confirm that:

  • I understand the purpose of Kaiend Care.
  • I understand that the application does not diagnose or independently provide medical advice.
  • I understand that the application is in its testing phase.
  • I agree to use the application responsibly.
  • I understand that my personal and health information will be processed subject to the applicable privacy requirements and notices.
  • I understand my rights under POPIA.
